# Code of Conduct

We want a respectful, accessible research and development community.

Participants must be considerate, use inclusive language, respect privacy and
research consent, and engage constructively with disagreement. Harassment,
threats, discrimination, doxxing, and publishing another person's private
material without permission are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these
expectations. Until a public project contact is established, report conduct
concerns privately to the repository owner through the hosting platform; do
not include manuscript contents or personal data in a public issue.
