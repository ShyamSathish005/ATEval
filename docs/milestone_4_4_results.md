# Milestone 4.4 results

## Status: human evaluation pending

M4.4 produced an isolated, blinded reviewer workflow and immutable 130-item
packet without introducing a generative model or modifying M4.1–M4.3 results.
The packet includes 30 diagnostic comparison items and 100 new necessity items.

No qualified reviewer annotations were available in this execution. It is
therefore not scientifically valid to claim that SCAR improves writing,
preserves meaning, or outperforms unrestricted generation. False acceptance,
false rejection, reviewer agreement, and primary endpoint estimates remain
pending.

The next decision is conditional: collect at least two independent reviewers
per comparison item, adjudicate technical disputes with a third reviewer, then
run the preregistered analysis before changing SCAR gates.
